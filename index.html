THE METHOD

Transparent by design.

This is a preseason screening model, not a black box. Action Network ranks become standardized team-strength inputs. NFL Analytics expected wins provide the prior. A discrete season distribution converts projected wins into over/under probabilities, then each price is evaluated against its raw break-even rate.

  1. NormalizeReverse ranks and standardize each factor across 32 teams.
  2. CombineApply editable weights; keep overall offense out to prevent double counting.
  3. ProjectBlend component strength with the independent prior and center league wins at 8.5.
  4. DistributeUse a continuity-corrected season-win distribution with editable uncertainty.
  5. PriceCompare model probability with the offered odds, not just the win-total line.
